What other players see

  • Public (anyone with your link): username, avatar, banner, bio, links, and for each game your region, country, city if you set one, platform, languages, mic, game details and when you were last active.
  • Only squadmates (after you both like each other): your in-game name and Discord.
  • Never shown: your email address.

Blocked players

Blocked players don't see you in matches, likes or squads in any game, and you don't see them. When you're logged in, their profile pages are hidden from you too.
