Using PlayMate

PlayMate helps players find teammates. Use it to meet people to play with — not to harass, spam, scam, impersonate others or share content that is hateful, sexual or illegal.

Your account

  • Keep your login details safe; you are responsible for activity on your account.
  • Your PlayMate username is public. Pick one that doesn't impersonate anyone.
  • We may suspend accounts that break these rules or are reported for abuse.

Games and trademarks

PlayMate is an independent service and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any game publisher. Game names, logos and cover artwork belong to their respective owners and are shown only to identify which games you play. Rights holders who want an image removed can contact us and we will take it down.

No guarantees

We do our best to keep PlayMate running and safe, but the service is provided "as is". Be careful when meeting people online and never share passwords or payment details.

Privacy

Draft — this policy will be finalised before PlayMate's public launch.

What we store

  • Account: your email address and a password (handled by our login provider, Supabase). Your email is never shown to other players.
  • Public profile: username, avatar, banner, bio and links you add, and for each game: region, country, city (optional), platform, languages, mic preference and game details like mode or rank.
  • Private until you match: your in-game name and Discord — only shown to players you have both liked.
  • Activity: who you liked, who you blocked or reported, when you were last active, and whether you turned on "Looking now".

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or show you third-party ads.
  • We don't read your messages on other apps — PlayMate only shares the names you choose to add.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile any time, remove a game profile, or block players.
  • Delete your account and all its data any time: Settings → Delete my account. Game profiles, likes, squads, blocks and your login are removed right away.
