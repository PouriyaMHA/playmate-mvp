Terms of use

PlayMate ("we") is a free service that helps gamers find teammates. By creating an account you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use PlayMate.

Who can use PlayMate

  • You must be at least 16 years old. If we learn that an account belongs to someone younger, we will delete it.
  • One account per person. Keep your login safe — you are responsible for what happens on your account.

Your profile and what others see

  • Your PlayMate username, avatar, banner, bio, links and your game profiles (region, country, optional city, platform, languages, mic and game details such as mode or rank) are public: anyone on the internet can see them, logged in or not. Don't put contact or personal details in your bio or links.
  • Your in-game name, Discord and Steam profile are shown only to players you have both liked ("squadmates").
  • Pick a username that doesn't impersonate anyone and isn't offensive.

Rules

Use PlayMate to find people to play with. Don't harass, threaten, scam, spam or impersonate anyone; don't share hateful, sexual or illegal content; don't use PlayMate to contact minors; don't collect other players' data or use bots or scripts against the service.

Blocking, reporting and enforcement

  • You can block or report any player. A block hides you from each other in matches, likes and squads in every game. It can't stop someone who isn't logged in from viewing your public profile, and it can't take back contact details a squadmate already copied.
  • Reports are reviewed by the PlayMate team. We may remove content, or limit or delete accounts that break these rules. We can't promise a reply to every report.

Other services

When you contact a squadmate on Discord, Steam or in a game, that happens outside PlayMate and under those services' rules. PlayMate is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any game publisher. Game names, logos and artwork belong to their owners and are shown only to identify the games; rights holders can ask us to remove an image.

No guarantees

PlayMate is provided "as is", free of charge. We work to keep it running and safe, but we can't guarantee that it will always be available, that you will find teammates, or how other players behave. Be careful when meeting people online and never share passwords or payment details. To the extent the law allows, we aren't liable for indirect losses arising from your use of PlayMate.

Changes and ending your account

We may update these terms; if a change is important we will show a notice in the app before it applies. You can stop using PlayMate and delete your account at any time (Settings → Delete my account).

Privacy policy

This policy explains what PlayMate collects, why, who can see it and the choices you have. We follow Canada's privacy law (PIPEDA) and, for players in Québec, Law 25.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password (stored by our login provider; we never see your password). Your email is never shown to other players.
  • Public profile: username, avatar, banner, bio, links, and for each game: region, country, optional city, platform, languages, mic preference and game details.
  • Private contact details: in-game name, Discord and Steam profile — shown only to your squadmates.
  • Activity: who you liked, blocked or reported, your squadmates, when you were last active, whether "Looking now" is on, and who invited you (if you came through an invite link).
  • Technical: our hosting and database providers keep standard server logs (such as IP address and the time of a request) to run and protect the service.

Why we use it

Only to run PlayMate: to match you with compatible players, show your profile, share contact details with squadmates, keep the service safe (blocks, reports, abuse limits) and fix problems. We don't sell your personal information, we don't show ads and we don't use it for advertising.

Who receives it

  • Other players see your public profile; squadmates also see your contact details.
  • Service providers that store or deliver data for us: Supabase (database and login) and Render (hosting, which uses Cloudflare). The app also loads a script library from unpkg. These providers process data on our behalf and may store it outside Canada, including in the United States, where it can be subject to local law.
  • We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Your browser

PlayMate doesn't use cookies for tracking or ads. To keep you logged in and remember your choices, the app saves a few items in your browser's local and session storage: your login session, the game you chose, a copy of your shared profile fields to pre-fill new games, an invite code, players you skipped in this session and the profile you came from. Logging out removes them.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. When you delete your account (Settings → Delete my account), your game profiles, likes, squads, blocks, reports you made or received, your username and your login are removed right away from the live service. Copies in our providers' backups and logs are deleted on their own schedules. Contact details a squadmate already copied can't be recalled.

Your rights

  • See and correct your information any time in the app, and ask us for a copy of what we hold about you.
  • Delete your account and data yourself in Settings.
  • Ask questions or make a complaint — contact us below. If you're not satisfied, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca) or, in Québec, the Commission d'accès à l'information.
